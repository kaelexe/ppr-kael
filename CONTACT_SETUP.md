# Contact options

Contact now presents a direct email invitation instead of a form. No delivery service, activation email, API key, or Gmail password is required.

- **Open in Gmail** opens a new tab with `johnmichaelamto@gmail.com` as the recipient and **Portfolio inquiry** as the subject. Visitors compose, review, and send their message in Gmail. Google may ask them to sign in.
- **Copy email** copies the visible address. Success is shown only after the clipboard write completes. If copying is unavailable or denied, visitors can select and copy the address manually.
- **Use your email app** uses a standard `mailto:` link for visitors who prefer another email provider or application. Their device must have an email handler configured.

The portfolio does not submit or send messages itself and cannot verify inbox delivery. The former FormSubmit setup is no longer used. Do not send test emails without the owner's authorization.
